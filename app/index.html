Loading the camera
  1. Needs firmware V103B / V105, or a Camp Snap 2. Not the Pro, CS-8 or 110D.
  2. Plug in by USB and copy one .flt to the root of the card. With several, the camera takes the first it finds.
  3. Power on. CUS flashes when the filter loads.

The preview replays the camera's pipeline (linear colour matrix → camera gamma → RGB curves) on an already-developed photo: close, not identical.
